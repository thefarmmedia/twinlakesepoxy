HOW IT WORKS

1
Tell Us What You're Hunting

Fill out the form below with as much detail as you have — series, issue number, era, condition, budget. The more we know, the better we can search.

2
We Search Our Inventory

Jeff searches our entire back issue stock — including stuff that hasn't been fully cataloged yet. We know our boxes better than our database.

3
We Hit Our Dealer Network

Not in stock? We reach out to our network of dealers, fellow shops, and regular sellers. If it's findable, we'll find it.

4
You Get The Call First

The moment a copy comes through our doors — whether from a collection we bought or a dealer find — you're the first call. Before it goes on the shelf.

🎯 Your Personal Hunt List

Every comic you submit goes on your personal hunt list. You're not just sending a form into a void — Jeff actually reads these and actively looks when new collections come in. You can add as many books as you want.

How It Plays Out

"I've been looking for Amazing Spider-Man #129 in VF for two years. I submit it on the Hunt List."

Six weeks later, Jeff buys a collection with a VF copy. Before it's bagged, tagged, or priced — your phone rings. "Hey, this is Jeff at Another Comic Shop. That ASM 129 you've been hunting? Just came in. Want me to hold it?"
📦 We Buy Collections Weekly

Because we actively buy collections from all over the Ozarks, new inventory comes through the door constantly — stuff that's never been online, never been on eBay. Hunt List subscribers get first crack at all of it.

Not sure what issue it is?

No problem. Tell us the character, approximate era, and anything you remember about the cover or story. We're collectors — we can usually figure it out.
